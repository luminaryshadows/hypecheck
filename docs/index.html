Do X posts improve memecoin forecasts?

We used yesterday’s archived posts to forecast whether 13 memecoins would rise. Adding post data reduced accuracy in this study.

Model estimates that the coin finishes up

Outcome hidden.

Up means the UTC day’s close is higher than its open. Market history uses earlier prices and volume. The models call up at 50% or higher. The estimates have no separate calibration check. Use the replay to compare forecasts with what happened.

How the forecasts performed

MethodAccuracyBalanced accuracyBrier error ↓

Accuracy counts correct calls. Balanced accuracy gives up and not-up days equal weight. Brier error measures how far the model’s probabilities were from what happened. Lower is better.

Results by post type

We used keyword rules to group posts. Each test adds one group’s post count and average sentiment to market history and scores it on every test row. All four intervals include zero.

What limits the result

The team examined these dates in earlier work. This study is exploratory. FinTwitBERT assigns tone to post wording, and the direction models also use post count and whether the archive had posts. A tagged coin can be a passing mention.
