# Exploratory analyses

> Archive of earlier experiments. See the [current 13-coin submission](../README.md) and its [method](method.md).

The five-coin historical replay was an earlier benchmark. The repository also retains archived experiments that examined different targets and coin universes. These records document hypotheses and failure modes. The five-coin direction and volatility studies use their earlier inputs and artifacts.

- The 23-coin same-day summary groups 1,749 observed coin-days and omits days without eligible posts. The coin universe used [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories/meme-token) classifications and Binance listings. It is retrospective and can be affected by listing selection and changing source coverage. In the committed summary, post count correlates 0.166 with absolute same-day open-to-close moves, and aggregate sentiment correlates 0.115 with signed moves. Price and posting windows overlap. Shared date shocks and repeated coins also make ordinary correlation p-values unsuitable as confirmation.
- The archived text model was trained on FinTwitBERT scores. Shuffled-fold agreement measures imitation of the teacher. The subsequent full-corpus refit makes the exported scores unsuitable as historical forecast inputs. The reported association of score magnitude with captured likes was near zero. The 23-coin results include earlier periods than the primary experiment.
- The archived minute study includes 3,145 coin–post matches and 2,926 distinct post IDs. The event minute contains trading on both sides of the timestamp. Forward returns overlap across events, and requiring all horizons before midnight excludes late posts. The earlier BTC placebo could sample different times for the coin and benchmark. Use these records as descriptive only.
- The richer post-feature study evaluates multiple feature blocks. Selecting the best block from the same folds makes its score exploratory. Repeat-author overlap is a concern when evaluating new-account generalization. A shared calendar date between training and test is a separate problem for forecast evaluation. Captured engagement is concentrated late in the data, and unknown authors cannot be treated as one account.
- The prior open-to-close, close-to-close, same-day range and minute-return results answer different questions. The archived Telegram and launchpad-trade CSVs are outside the five-coin submission. The archived call table was attributed in Git history to [Solana Memecoin Calls on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/smugcalls/solana-memecoin-calls). Redistribution terms have not been verified.

The new five-coin volatility comparison uses the complete calendar, includes zero-observation days, uses lagged market features and runs on the main experiment's purged partitions. Run `python vol_model.py` to reproduce its output.
